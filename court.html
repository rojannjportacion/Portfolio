Barangay Court

You're RJ. Walk into the ball to grab it. Hold the action button and let go in the green. Standing still is a jump shot, moving away from the hoop is a fadeaway, moving toward it is a runner, and running at the rim is a dunk (keep holding to hang on). Tap action without the ball to jump for rebounds.

Talk to Tito Randy for a game of HORSE, or ask Han in the front row to rebound for you. Hit 3, 5 and 10 jumpers in a row and see what happens.

Keyboard: arrow keys or A / D to move, E or Space to act.

Barangay dictionary

Barangay
The smallest unit of local government in the Philippines, roughly a neighborhood. Almost every one has a covered court.
Tito
"Uncle," but used for any older man you respect, related or not.
Merienda
The afternoon snack. Losers buy it.
Taho
Warm silken tofu with brown-sugar syrup and sago pearls, sold by vendors who call "Tahooo!" down the street.
Jeepney
Brightly painted public minibuses built from old army jeeps.
Mabuhay
"Long live," the classic Filipino welcome.
Puso
"Heart." The rallying cry of Philippine basketball.
Liga
The neighborhood basketball league, the center of summer life.
Chismis / Marites
Gossip, and the nickname for someone who loves it.
Lodi / Petmalu
Slang for "idol" and "awesome," spelled backwards.
Sayang / Ay nako
"What a waste!" and "Oh, my goodness."
Bawal tambay
"No loitering," which everyone ignores.
